## About Me

I am **Selim Ben Abdelkader**, a software engineering student specializing in **FinTech** at **ESPRIT (Tunisia)**, currently in the engineering cycle.

I am focused on **digital payments engineering**: card and electronic payment infrastructure, digital wallets, NFC, transaction security and fraud detection. I am building public projects to turn what I study and what I observed in the field into code I can explain and defend.

**Field exposure**

- **UIB (Union Internationale de Banques)**, Cards & Payments (Monétique) department, summer 2026: immersion internship covering digital payment studies, digital wallets, NFC prototypes and financial cybersecurity.
- **MS Solutions**, July 2024: observation internship in electronic payment solutions and transaction security.

**Goal:** an international double-degree program, then an international career in FinTech engineering.
